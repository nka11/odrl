Abstract

This specification defines the core of the Dialectical Ethical Profile for ODRL 1.0 (hegel-odrl), an extension profile of the W3C Open Digital Rights Language (ODRL) 2.2 Information Model and Vocabulary [ODRL-MODEL] [ODRL-VOCAB]. Synchronous, deterministic policy evaluation works for operational rules (geofencing, authentication, quotas) but collapses for substantive AI and data ethics commitments—algorithmic fairness, explainability, human oversight—that cannot be verified in milliseconds at an enforcement point.

Drawing on G. W. F. Hegel's dialectical synthesis (Aufhebung) of positive law (Creon / Nomos) and substantive ethics (Antigone / Ágraphoi Nómoi), the core decouples synchronous enforcement from asynchronous ethical governance. It defines the hegel: namespace (hegel:EthicalCommitment, a six-state asynchronous Policy Decision Point machine, inspection oracles, deliberation and remedies) and the domain-neutral part of the eth: substantive ethics vocabulary (data and algorithmic ethics). It deliberately prescribes no identity scheme, credential format, revocation registry or execution environment: these are supplied by separate binding profiles.

It defines the core that deployment-specific binding profiles specialise. Contributions, ontology reviews, and reference implementation feedback are invited.

1.Introduction

ODRL 2.2 is the canonical W3C language for expressing what is permitted, prohibited, or obligated over digital assets [ODRL-MODEL]. Digital ecosystems that share data and AI models under such policies need to govern not only access but also how assets are subsequently used.

Historically, policy engines (XACML and standard ODRL profiles) have operated under an implicit dogma of synchronous Boolean reductionism: every policy rule must evaluate to true or false within microseconds at a Policy Enforcement Point (PEP) using operators such as eq, gt, or lteq. When applied to modern AI pipelines, this dogma produces an ethical collapse: because a network gateway cannot deterministically verify in 5 milliseconds whether a downstream neural network will exhibit demographic bias or comply with a platform carbon-intensity ceiling, traditional implementations either blindly block legitimate data sharing or omit ethical clauses from machine-readable contracts altogether.

1.1Philosophical & Architectural Foundation

To resolve this structural impasse, this profile adopts the dialectical architecture derived from G. W. F. Hegel's analysis of Sophocles' Antigone in the Phenomenology of Spirit [HEGEL-PS]:

  • The Creon Layer (Nomos / Positive Law): Synchronous, deterministic, immediately enforceable rules (odrl:Permission, odrl:Prohibition, odrl:Duty with computable odrl:Constraints) evaluated at the gateway PEP in ≤ 5 ms. Guarantees operational certainty, identity verification, geofencing, and quotas.
  • The Antigone Layer (Ágraphoi Nómoi / Substantive Ethics): Qualitative, contextual, and emergent obligations encapsulated in hegel:EthicalCommitment. Evaluated asynchronously by inspection oracles (hegel:inspectionSource) and deliberative bodies (hegel:deliberationTarget) through cryptographic adhesion, continuous provenance telemetry, and ex-post forensic audits.
  • The Dialectical Synthesis (Aufhebung): Preserves the ultra-low latency of the Creon gateway while elevating Antigone's ethical principles into binding, auditable, and ultimately coercible artifacts across the data lifecycle.

1.2Progressive Expressivity vs. Immediate Enforcement

Design Principle: Expressivity Before Enforcement Not all ethical commitments expressed using this profile are immediately enforceable via automated runtime verification today. Formalizing ethical requirements inside ODRL contracts is a prerequisite for accountability: consumers consciously review and cryptographically sign these commitments upon contract acceptance, establishing legal traceability and enabling progressive automation as hardware enclaves (TEEs), statistical oracles, and verifiable unlearning mature. If an ethical requirement is never expressed in the policy, it can never become verifiable.

2.Conformance & Namespaces

As well as sections marked as non-normative, all authoring guidelines, diagrams, examples, and notes in this specification are non-normative. Everything else in this specification is normative.

The key words MAY, MUST, MUST NOT, OPTIONAL, RECOMMENDED, REQUIRED, SHALL, and SHOULD in this document are to be interpreted as described in BCP 14 [RFC2119] [RFC8174] when, and only when, they appear in all capitals, as shown here.

A conforming Dialectical ODRL Policy (core) MUST conform to the ODRL Information Model 2.2 [ODRL-MODEL] and ODRL Profile Best Practices [ODRL-BP], MUST declare the profile URI https://w3id.org/hegel-odrl/profile/core/v1/ via the odrl:profile property, and MUST NOT place non-deterministic substantive ethical predicates inside synchronous odrl:Permission constraints unless an external certified attestation oracle is explicitly bound.

A binding profile specialises this core for a concrete deployment context. It MUST declare itself a profile of the core (prof:isProfileOf) and MUST satisfy the requirements of Section 5.

3.Information Model Extensions (hegel:)

In accordance with the Profile Mechanism (Section 3.3) of the ODRL Information Model 2.2 [ODRL-MODEL], this profile introduces a new subclass of odrl:Rule to isolate qualitative ethical commitments from synchronous operational rules without breaking backwards compatibility with standard ODRL parsers.

3.1The hegel:EthicalCommitment Class

hegel:EthicalCommitment Antigone Layer

Identifier (URI):
https://w3id.org/hegel-odrl/ns/hegel/EthicalCommitment
Type:
owl:Class, rdfs:Class
SubClass Of:
odrl:Rule
Definition:
A normative rule binding an odrl:assignee to substantive ethical obligations or abstentions over a target asset (or artifacts derived therefrom) that cannot be deterministically evaluated during synchronous network transit.
Normative Behavior:
A Policy containing one or more hegel:EthicalCommitment rules MUST NOT grant hegel:PERMIT_UNCONDITIONAL. The PDP MUST require ex-ante attestation (hegel:attestation) and transition execution into hegel:AUDIT_PENDING under telemetry supervision.

The core also defines the following supporting classes:

hegel:PDPStateA state of the six-state asynchronous PDP automaton (Section 3.3).
hegel:DeliberativeBodySubclass of odrl:Party: an ethics committee or HITL arbitration authority.
hegel:DeliberationDossierEvidence package escalated to a hegel:DeliberativeBody.
hegel:AttestationAbstract verifiable statement by a party; concrete bindings specialise it.
hegel:EthicalAdherenceAttestationEx-ante attestation by the assignee accepting the referenced commitments.
hegel:InspectionReportEx-post verdict issued by an inspection oracle.

3.3Asynchronous PDP State Machine (hegel:PDPState)

Conforming Policy Decision Points MUST implement a six-state asynchronous finite-state automaton transcending binary Permit/Deny evaluation:

State URI (hegel:) Layer Entry Guard Condition Allowed Outbound Transitions
hegel:DENY Creon Violation of any synchronous deterministic constraint (odrl:Prohibition, expired quota, invalid token) or revocation of the assignee's attestation. Terminal state for current request.
hegel:PERMIT_UNCONDITIONAL Creon All synchronous Creon constraints satisfied AND zero hegel:EthicalCommitment rules present in the Agreement. Standard data transfer completion.
hegel:PROVISIONAL_PERMIT_ATTESTED Synthesis Creon constraints satisfied, hegel:EthicalCommitment present, a valid hegel:Attestation verified and not revoked. hegel:AUDIT_PENDING (on execution start); hegel:SUSPENDED_DELIBERATION.
hegel:AUDIT_PENDING Antigone Active processing/training phase. Continuous W3C PROV-O telemetry streams to hegel:inspectionSource; derived artifacts held under hegel:cryptographicLease. Completed Audit (lease released); hegel:SUSPENDED_DELIBERATION (risk alert); hegel:REVOKED_ETHICAL_BREACH (confirmed breach).
hegel:SUSPENDED_DELIBERATION Antigone Normative conflict, ambiguity, or hegel:predictiveRiskScore breach. Access frozen; hegel:DeliberationDossier escalated to hegel:deliberationTarget (HITL). hegel:PROVISIONAL_PERMIT_ATTESTED (favorable arbitral ruling); hegel:DENY (rejection); hegel:REVOKED_ETHICAL_BREACH.
hegel:REVOKED_ETHICAL_BREACH Synthesis Empirical forensic proof in PROV-O (or arbitral ruling) confirming violation of ethical thresholds. Triggers revocation of the assignee's attestation and hegel:remedyProtocol. Terminal sanction (mandatory eth:machineUnlearning & artifact invalidation).

3.4The Aufhebung Enforcement Loop

4.Substantive Ethics Vocabulary (eth:)

The eth: namespace defines actions, left operands, and controlled right operands for the Data and Algorithm ethical dimensions plus end-of-use and remediation controls. Binding profiles MAY extend it with further dimensions. Terms marked Extended Action represent recommended lifecycle extensions added for operational completeness.

4.1Actions (odrl:Action)

All actions below are instances of odrl:Action and skos:Concept, refining core ODRL actions (such as odrl:use, odrl:transform, odrl:inform, or odrl:delete) via odrl:includedIn.

eth:anonymize odrl:anonymize Duty (Pre-processing) Obligation to apply mathematical obfuscation or privacy-preserving transformations (e.g., differential privacy, k-anonymity) prior to downstream processing.

5.Binding Requirements

The core states what must be verified asynchronously, not how. A binding profile MUST define each of the following, and MUST NOT alter the semantics of the PDP states of Section 3.3:

ConcernCore abstractionBinding MUST define
Party identificationodrl:Party, hegel:inspectionSource, hegel:deliberationTargetThe identifier scheme and how parties are resolved and trusted.
Ex-ante adhesionhegel:EthicalAdherenceAttestation, eth:attestAdherenceA verifiable, policy-bound attestation format and the verification procedure preceding hegel:PROVISIONAL_PERMIT_ATTESTED.
Ex-post verdicthegel:InspectionReportA verifiable report format carrying the verdict state and triggered remedy.
RevocationTransition to hegel:REVOKED_ETHICAL_BREACH then hegel:DENYA registry that the synchronous enforcement point can query in bounded time without running the inspection itself.
Telemetryhegel:forensicEvidence, eth:emitProvenanceThe channel and integrity protection for PROV-O lineage during hegel:AUDIT_PENDING.
Artifact escrowhegel:cryptographicLease, eth:quarantineArtifactThe isolation mechanism preventing export of derived artifacts before audit clearance.
Deliberation trust anchorhegel:DeliberativeBodyHow the authority and decision rules of the deliberative body are published and verified.

6.Normative Profile Examples

The examples draw on creative and media use cases, the kind of AI-related rights scenarios addressed by the ODRL AI vocabulary: a photo agency licensing its library for image generation, an artists' collective licensing illustrations for fine-tuning, and a voice actor licensing recordings for speech synthesis. They are deployment-neutral: parties, assets and evidence are plain http://example.org/ URIs, and nothing depends on a particular identity scheme, credential format or execution environment (see Section 5). The context.jsonld context defines the hegel:, eth:, prov: and xsd: prefixes and the value coercions of the hegel: properties.

6.1Anti-Pattern: Naive Synchronous Ethical Policy (Non-Conforming)

A stock photo agency licenses its photo library to an image-generation lab.

Non-Conforming Pattern (Creon Collapse) Placing emergent ethical checks (eth:demographicParityDiff, eth:humanOversight) directly inside a synchronous odrl:Permission constraint block forces a millisecond gateway PEP to evaluate how the trained model will later represent people, a non-deterministic downstream property, causing silent failure or Indeterminate denial.

6.2Minimal Conforming Agreement (JSON-LD)

An artists' collective licenses its illustrations to a game studio for fine-tuning an image model. The Agreement keeps the synchronous rule (a permission with no ethical predicate) separate from the asynchronous hegel:EthicalCommitment. The permission only requires the studio to attest adherence (eth:attestAdherence); the oversight level and the fairness of how the model depicts people are verified later by the hegel:inspectionSource. The commitment names the action it governs and the PROV-O collection that receives the training lineage. On breach, the collective's illustrations must be unlearned from the model.

6.3Oracle Risk Bound, Deliberation and Remedies (JSON-LD)

A voice actor licenses studio recordings to a speech-synthesis company. The commitment governs generating speech with the derived voice model and prohibits dual use such as impersonation (eth:dualUseProhibition). The oracle's asynchronous hegel:predictiveRiskScore (likelihood that outputs imitate a real person without consent) and hegel:driftForecast are bounded by constraints. If the risk score exceeds 0.70 the duty eth:escalateDeliberation applies and the Agreement enters hegel:SUSPENDED_DELIBERATION, referred to the actor's talent panel. A confirmed breach enters hegel:REVOKED_ETHICAL_BREACH and triggers hegel:remedyProtocol: invalidation of the synthetic voices produced, then machine unlearning of the actor's voice.

6.4Agreement Lifecycle State (JSON-LD)

The PDP records the state of an instantiated Agreement with hegel:pdpState. For the Agreement of Section 6.3 a typical lifecycle is: hegel:PROVISIONAL_PERMIT_ATTESTED once the adherence attestation is verified; hegel:AUDIT_PENDING when synthesis starts; hegel:SUSPENDED_DELIBERATION when the risk bound is exceeded; then either back to hegel:PROVISIONAL_PERMIT_ATTESTED (favorable ruling) or hegel:DENY / hegel:REVOKED_ETHICAL_BREACH. The node below records the Agreement while the risk bound is exceeded.

  "hegel:pdpState": "hegel:SUSPENDED_DELIBERATION"
}

7.Security, Privacy & Governance Considerations

  • Exfiltration Race Conditions: Asynchronous auditing introduces a time-of-check to time-of-use (TOCTOU) window between execution and forensic verdict. Policies handling high-impact assets SHOULD pair eth:quarantineArtifact with a bounded hegel:cryptographicLease; the binding profile defines the isolation mechanism that makes the lease enforceable.
  • Telemetry Overhead vs. Audit Granularity: Continuous PROV-O emission can degrade high-throughput pipelines. Implementations SHOULD batch PROV-O metadata at checkpoint boundaries while keeping batch intervals inside the lease window.
  • Privacy-Preserving Auditing: Inspection oracles MUST operate over aggregated statistical summaries, confusion matrices, attestations, and PROV-O structural metadata rather than raw personal data, preserving data minimization under GDPR.
  • Trust Anchor for Deliberation: Deployments implementing hegel:SUSPENDED_DELIBERATION MUST make the identity and decision rules of the hegel:deliberationTarget verifiable by all participants, as required by Section 5.

B.Formal Deontic Positioning (Informative)

Standard Deontic Logic (SDL / modal system KD) enforces consistency axiom D (¬(O(p) ∧ O(¬p))), causing deductive explosion when faced with tragic collisions between positive law and substantive ethics, and failing on Contrary-to-Duty (CTD) obligations such as Chisholm's Paradox. Defeasible Deontic Logic (DDL) resolves static contradictions through pre-declared superiority relations (r1 > r2) and reparative chains, but remains bound to discrete propositional rules decided at design time.

The hegel-odrl profile operationalizes a temporalized, process-oriented deontic architecture: unresolved normative collisions transition cleanly into hegel:SUSPENDED_DELIBERATION (avoiding arbitrary static override), while primary ethical violations detected asynchronously in hegel:AUDIT_PENDING deterministically enact CTD remedial duties (hegel:remedyProtocol → eth:machineUnlearning) backed by cryptographic revocation.
